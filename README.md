# About Me

I am a Software Engineering graduate at WeThinkCode_.  
I’m the founder of **Vennor**, a modern ecommerce platform built to empower small businesses.

I discovered programming (Computer Science) at Wits through a friend, Anitha Tyopo. What started as curiosity quickly became a commitment to building systems that are reliable, scalable, and useful in the real world.

Today I work across cloud infrastructure and product engineering as an AWS Cloud Engineer and Full Stack Developer. I enjoy taking ideas from zero to production and designing software that can grow with its users.

My interests include distributed systems, platform architecture, and the future of digital commerce.

---

## What I’m Focused On

- Building scalable, production-ready web & mobile platforms  
- Designing secure and observable cloud infrastructure  
- Creating tools that lower the barrier for entrepreneurs to participate in the digital economy  
- Continuously improving engineering discipline, testing, and delivery speed
